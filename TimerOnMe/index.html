Every Timer, Alarm & Stopwatch — At Once

Run up to 50 timers and stopwatches at the same time, set alarms that ring even when your iPhone is locked, and time HIIT workouts with precision — on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to master your time

Multi-Timer Grid

Run up to 50 independent timers and stopwatches at once in a clean grid. Start, pause, and reset each one individually — or control them all together.

Reliable Alarms

Wake-up and reminder alarms with custom labels and weekday schedules, powered by AlarmKit so they ring even when your iPhone is locked or Timer on Me is closed. Snooze options and your own sounds included.

Interval Training

Built-in Tabata, EMOM, and 30/30 presets get you training in two taps, plus a custom interval editor and a full-screen workout mode with color cues.

Apple Watch App

A full native Apple Watch app — run multiple stopwatches and timers on your wrist with centisecond precision and complications, even when your iPhone isn't around.

Widgets & Live Activities

Control your timers from the Home Screen with interactive widgets, and watch the countdown from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with Live Activities.

Alarm Sounds, Your Way

Choose from a dozen built-in alarm sounds or import your own. Your alerts ring even when your iPhone is locked, silenced, or the app is closed.

Session Management

Save your timer setups and load them anytime. Export sessions as JSON, CSV, or text for easy sharing and backup.

Smart Display

Centisecond precision, percentage progress, and a keep-screen-on mode. Customize how your timers look and behave.
