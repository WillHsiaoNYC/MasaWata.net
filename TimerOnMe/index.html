Multiple Timers, One App

Run as many timers and stopwatches as you need, all at once. Perfect for cooking, workouts, studying, or anything that needs precise timing.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to master your time

Multi-Timer Grid

Run multiple timers and stopwatches simultaneously in a clean, responsive grid. Each clock has independent controls—start, pause, and reset individually.

Interval Training

Built-in HIIT, Tabata, and custom interval presets. Create your own work/rest sequences for the perfect workout routine.

Home Screen Widgets

Control your timers right from your home screen. Small, medium, and large widget sizes to fit your layout.

Session Management

Save your timer setups and load them anytime. Export sessions as JSON, CSV, or text for easy sharing and backup.

Custom Ringtones

Choose from 7 built-in alarm sounds—from a simple bell to vintage phone rings. Alarms work even in Silent Mode with iOS 18.2+.

Smart Display

Centisecond precision, percentage progress, and a keep-screen-on mode. Customize how your timers look and behave.
