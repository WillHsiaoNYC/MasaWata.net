A few captures worth keeping

  1. Find your spot

    Stand still on a safe public sidewalk, clear of traffic and entrances. Keep your phone pointed toward passing cars.

  2. Save three readable plates

    Use the live scanner, check each reading, and correct it when needed before keeping the capture.

  3. Revisit your collection

    Open Plates to look back through the photographs you saved, then mark a favorite.

This is a personal three-capture goal during the event week. Recognition depends on plate format, distance, speed, and image conditions. There are no prizes or rankings.

Stay on the sidewalk, respect private property and others’ privacy, and never use the app while driving.
