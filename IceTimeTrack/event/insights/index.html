Post-skate Insights Launch

Compare ice time, heart rate, and active calories.

  1. Open Dashboard on your iPhone.
  2. Choose a completed session recorded with Apple Watch.
  3. Expand Post-skate insight to compare your recorded sessions.

For older sessions, choose a month in the calendar. Groups compare sessions of the same type.

Requires Ice Time Track 1.8 or later.
